1) What ROLLUP Does ?

ROLLUP adds:

*) Sub total per department
*) Grand total (all departments)


2) What CUBE Does ?

CUBE gives:

*) Every All combinations total 
   Ex :
    By department + gender
    By department only
    By gender only

*) Grand total


3) Difference between ROLLUP and CUBE ?

| Feature     | ROLLUP           | CUBE             |
| ----------- | ---------------- | ---------------- |
| Subtotals   | Hierarchical     | All combinations |
| Complexity  | Simple           | More rows        |
| Performance | Faster           | Slower           |
| Use Case    | Reports by level | Analytics        |


ROLLUP = Top → Down totals
CUBE = Every possible total
