1) How do you identify a slow query?

	*) I would confirm whether the slowdown is caused by the query itself or by the database environment
	*) I identify queries with high execution time,
	*) Analyzing execution plans using EXPLAIN  to understand where the query is spending most of its time
	*) I look for 
	        Avoid SELECT *
	        Filter early (predicate pushdown)
			Inefficient sorts
            Join on indexed columns 
            partition pruning the large table
	*) I also look for transaction block and dead lock and live lock

3) How would you optimize queries when tables grow from thousands to billions of records?
        
        When tables grow from thousands to billions of rows, 
        I focus on 

	        Avoid SELECT *
	        Filter early (predicate pushdown)
			Inefficient sorts
            Join on indexed columns 
            partition pruning the large table



4) What would you check in an execution plan? 

	I would check the execution plan for 
		1) expensive operators
		2) table or index scans 
		3) join strategies, 
		4) missing indexes
		4) estimated versus actual row counts, key lookups, sorts, spills and warnings
